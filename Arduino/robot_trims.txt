Robot servo trims, as printed by the robot at boot ("TRIMS: ..." on its USB serial).
Recorded 2026-09-30 from firmware #RV3r1c-Chris10-0.01, unchanged across several boots and
across the firmware flashes made that day, so they are the values stored in the robot's EEPROM.

Servo 0..11:  22 14 -11 10 4 0 -2 6 18 6 -15 18

This is a written-down copy only. It is NOT an EEPROM dump: the EEPROM layout is address 0 = 'V'
(valid marker), addresses 1..12 = the trims, stored offset by TRIM_ZERO in the robot sketch
(the boot line prints value minus TRIM_ZERO). A real dump needs the robot's USB adapter plugged in.
