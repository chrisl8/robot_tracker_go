Calibration tags: print sheet

Five tags, one per page: Center and Corner 1-4. The calibration wizard tells you where to put them.

  1. Print at 100% / "Actual size". Do not use "Fit to page" or "Scale to fit". Letter and A4 both work.
  2. Use plain matte paper (glossy paper causes glare the camera cannot read).
  3. On every page, check that the scale bar measures 10 cm and that the black square measures 15 cm across. If not, fix the print scale and reprint that page.
  4. Cut off the page margins and tape each tag flat on the floor (no curling or wrinkles).
  5. The arrow on each tag marks its UP side: point it toward the top of the camera image.

The tags only need to be on the floor while you calibrate. Pick them up afterwards and use them again only if the camera moves or its resolution changes.
